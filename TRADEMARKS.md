# Wayfinder name and logo (draft for the maintainer to review)

The code is Apache-2.0: you may use, change, sell and redistribute it. Apache-2.0 does not give anyone the right to
use the project's name or logo (section 6 of the licence). This note says what that means in practice.

**You may, without asking:** say your software is "based on Wayfinder" or "compatible with Wayfinder"; describe the
project accurately; use the name when you distribute the unmodified official build.

**Please ask first before:** naming a changed version "Wayfinder"; using the name or logo in a product's marketing,
a school or district purchase listing, or an app-store listing; implying the project endorses you.

**Why:** Wayfinder is built so that students, especially neurodivergent students, can have it for free and keep their
data on their own device. The name tells a school what it is getting. A fork under another name is welcome; a
look-alike that charges students or collects their data under the Wayfinder name is not.

**The official free build:** the build published by the maintainers stays free to students, works without an
account, and keeps student data on the device unless the student opts in. This is a pledge, not a licence term.
